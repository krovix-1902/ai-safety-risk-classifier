# Research Question

**Can a simple, interpretable decision tree classify potential AI safety risks from observable interaction features?**

## Hypothesis

A shallow decision tree should provide useful baseline risk separation while remaining easy to inspect and explain.

## Why interpretability matters

A risk-triage system should make its decision process inspectable. A tree provides explicit rules rather than an opaque score.

## What would make the experiment stronger?

The current dataset is synthetic. A stronger study would use independently labelled examples, multiple annotators, separate train/test sources, robustness tests and analysis of false negatives.
